Mahindra XUV 3XO

Overview

The Mahindra XUV 3XO is a refreshed version of the XUV300, a subcompact SUV known for its bold exterior, premium interior, and spacious cabin. It offers a combination of style, comfort, functionality, and safety, making it suitable for urban families and adventure lovers. It has a 5-star Bharat NCAP safety rating.

Pricing

The XUV 3XO price ranges from approximately Rs. 7.99 Lakh to Rs. 15.80 Lakh (ex-showroom). Pricing varies depending on the variant and location. Different variants cater to various budgets.

Specifications

Engine

Available in petrol and diesel engine options with manual and automatic transmissions.

Petrol

  • 1.2L MPFi Turbo Petrol (110 PS, 200 Nm)
  • 1.2L TGDi Turbo Petrol (130 PS, 230 Nm)

Diesel

  • 1.5L Diesel (117 PS, 300 Nm)

Mileage

  • Petrol: 17.96 - 18.89 km/l
  • Diesel: 20.6 - 21.2 km/l

Dimensions

Length: 3990 mm

Width: 1821 mm

Height: 1647 mm

Wheelbase: 2600 mm

Boot Space: 295 litres

Features

Exterior

Aggressive looks, sharp LED headlamps, sleek grille, muscular stance, 17-inch diamond-cut alloy wheels, and connected LED tail lamps. Ground clearance of 201 mm.

  • LED DRLs
  • Infinity tail lamps
  • 17-inch diamond-cut alloy wheels

Interior

Luxurious cabin with premium materials, ergonomic seating, and top-notch infotainment options. Features Skyroof, soft-touch leatherette, premium leatherette seats, cooled glovebox, passive keyless entry, and front armrest with storage.

  • Skyroof
  • Soft-touch leatherette
  • Premium leatherette seats
  • Cooled glovebox
  • Passive keyless entry

Technology

Harman Kardon 7-speaker audio system, high-definition touchscreen, built-in Amazon Alexa, and ADRENOX Connect technology. Dual 26.03 cm HD infotainment and digital displays, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility.

  • Harman Kardon 7-speaker audio system
  • High-definition touchscreen
  • Amazon Alexa integration
  • ADRENOX Connect
  • Dual-zone climate control
  • Wireless charging

Safety

Safety features include 6 airbags as standard, ABS with EBD, rear parking sensors, all-wheel disc brakes, reverse parking camera (higher variants), Electronic Stability Programme, Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (higher variants), ISOFIX mounts, Hill Hold Assist, and Hill Descent Control.

  • 6 Airbags (standard)
  • ABS with EBD
  • Rear Parking Sensors
  • All Wheel Disc Brakes
  • Electronic Stability Programme
  • Tyre Pressure Monitoring System
  • ISOFIX Mounts
  • Hill Hold Assist
  • Hill Descent Control
