Mahindra XUV 3XO Detailed Report

Mahindra XUV 3XO is a compact SUV with prices ranging from Rs. 7.49 Lakh to Rs. 15.80 Lakh.
It comes in multiple variants, including MX1, MX2, MX2 PRO, MX3, MX3 PRO, AX5, AX5 Luxury, AX7, and AX7 Luxury.
The exterior features a bold design with LED DRLs, headlamps, and infinity tail lamps, along with 17-inch diamond-cut alloy wheels.
The interior offers a comfortable and sophisticated cabin with a Skyroof, soft-touch leatherette, premium leatherette seats, a cooled glovebox, and passive keyless entry.
Smart connectivity features include a Harman Kardon 7-speaker audio system, a high-definition touchscreen system for infotainment and navigation, and built-in Amazon Alexa.
The XUV 3XO is equipped with 35 standard safety features, including 6 airbags, ESC with Hill Hold Control, and all 4 disc brakes, engineered to meet high safety standards.
Available engine options include mStallion Turbo Charged Multipoint Fuel Injection (TCMPFi), mStallion Turbo Charged Intercooled Gasoline Direct injection (TGDi), and Turbo Diesel with CRDe engine.
It offers a 6-speed torque converter automatic transmission and smart steering modes, along with drive modes like Zip, Zap, and Zoom.
The XUV 3XO has a wheelbase of 2,600 mm and a cargo capacity of 364L, further expanded with 60:40 split seats.
Advanced technology features include Level 2 ADAS, dual-zone climate control, 65W USB-C charging, electronic parking brake with Auto Hold, and a 360-degree surround view system.
The vehicle also has connected car features via Adrenox Connect, offering control over 80+ features through a smartphone.
Fuel efficiency ranges from 17.96 to 20.1 kmpl for petrol and 20.6 to 21.2 kmpl for diesel engines.
New REVX variants introduced with 1.2-litre turbo-petrol engine options.
