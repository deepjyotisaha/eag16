Mahindra XUV 3XO Report

Feature Details

  • Compact SUV with a refreshed design and technology upgrades.
  • Available in multiple variants with petrol and diesel engine options.
  • Key features include LED DRLs, infinity tail lamps, and 17-inch diamond-cut alloy wheels.
  • Offers ample ground clearance of 201 mm for varied terrains.
  • Interior features include a Skyroof, soft-touch leatherette, and premium leatherette seats.

Digital Features

  • Harman Kardon 7-speaker audio system.
  • High-definition touchscreen infotainment system with navigation.
  • Built-in Amazon Alexa for entertainment and control.
  • Adrenox Connect technology for controlling car features via smartphone (80+ features).
  • Dual 10.25-inch screens for infotainment and digital cluster in higher variants.
  • Wireless Android Auto and wired Apple CarPlay in MX3 variant and above.
  • First-in-segment smart steering modes standard for all variants.
  • Wireless charging available in MX3 and above variants.
  • Dual-zone climate control with rear AC vents in top-spec versions.

Safety Features

  • 35 standard safety features.
  • 4 disc brakes as standard.
  • Level 2 Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS) with traffic sign recognition, high beam assist, lane departure warning, and smart pilot assist.
  • 6 airbags standard on all variants.
  • Electronic Stability Control (ESC).
  • 360-degree surround view system with blind view monitor in AX5 Luxury variant.
  • ISOFIX child seat mounts.
  • Hill assist.
  • Electronic brakeforce distribution.
  • Speed-sensing auto door lock.
  • 5-star Bharat NCAP rating

Pricing Information

  • Ex-showroom price ranges from Rs. 7.99 Lakh to Rs. 15.80 Lakh.
  • MX1 base model starts at Rs. 7.99 Lakh.
  • AX7L top model priced at Rs. 15.80 Lakh.
  • Prices vary based on variant and location.
