Mercedes S Class Features

Safety Features

Active Safety Systems

    Active Safety Systems

    • Active Brake Assist: Reduces vehicle speed to avoid or mitigate accidents.
    • Automatic Emergency Braking: Automatically applies brakes to prevent collisions.
    • Lane Departure Warning: Alerts the driver if the vehicle drifts out of its lane.
    • Blind Spot Monitoring: Detects and alerts the driver to vehicles in blind spots.
    • Adaptive Cruise Control: Maintains a safe distance from the vehicle ahead.
    • Crosswind Assist: Adjusts steering to maintain stability in strong winds.
    • Adaptive Highbeam Assist: Automatically adjusts headlight beams to enhance visibility without blinding other drivers.
    • Traffic Sign Assist: Recognizes and displays road signs to help drivers adhere to limits.

    Passive Safety Systems

    • Advanced Airbags: Multiple airbags throughout the cabin to protect occupants.
    • Reinforced Steel Body: High-strength steel and reinforced body structure for enhanced protection in crashes.
    • Impact-Absorbing Interior: Interior designed to absorb impacts and minimize force transferred to passengers.
    • Pre-Safe System: Detects potential collisions and prepares the vehicle by tightening seat belts and adjusting seating positions.
    • Intelligent bodyshell design: New aluminium hybrid bodyshell with an aluminium content exceeding 50 percent meets numerous requirements
    • Passenger cell: protected by ultra high-strength and press-hardened steel
    • The Mercedes-Benz safety cell is the centrepiece of the safety system.
